## Why Australian businesses are making the switch

Xero grew popular because it entered the market early and marketed well. But MYOB has spent the past few years rebuilding its platform from the ground up. The result: better features, lower costs, and tools designed for Australian businesses first, not adapted from overseas models.

If you're paying more each year for Xero while your needs stay the same, you're not alone. Many Australian businesses are switching to MYOB and finding they get more for less.

Here are five reasons why you should consider making the switch in 2026.

### 1. Better Australian tax compliance

MYOB builds its features around Australian tax law first. When Single Touch Payroll Phase 2 rolled out, MYOB had the changes ready before the deadline. When the ATO updates reporting requirements, MYOB updates the software within weeks.

Xero treats Australia as one market among many. Updates take longer. Support staff often need to escalate Australian-specific questions because the first-line team doesn't know local rules.

MYOB's BAS reporting pulls data directly from your transactions with less manual adjustment. You spend less time double-checking figures and more time running your business.

**Example:** A Melbourne-based construction company switched to MYOB and cut their BAS preparation time from 4 hours to 90 minutes. Their accountant confirmed the reports matched exactly what they needed for the ATO.

### 2. Lower costs for the features you need

Xero's pricing tiers force you to upgrade if you need payroll for more than 5 employees or want to track inventory properly. MYOB bundles these features into mid-tier plans at lower prices.

Here's what you pay annually:
- Xero Established (for growing businesses): $1,100/year
- MYOB Business Plus (comparable features): $840/year

You save $260 per year. That's $1,300 over five years for the same functionality.

MYOB doesn't charge extra for phone support. Xero makes you pay for priority support or wait in queue for basic help.

### 3. Payroll that handles Australian awards correctly

Australian award rates change every July. MYOB updates all award templates automatically. You don't need to manually adjust rates or worry about underpaying staff.

MYOB's payroll engine calculates penalties, allowances, and overtime based on Modern Awards. It handles split shifts, broken shifts, and weekend rates without custom formulas.

Xero requires more manual setup for complex awards. You'll spend time creating pay templates and checking calculations against the Fair Work website.

**Example:** A retail business with 15 staff on the General Retail Industry Award switched to MYOB. They stopped spending 2 hours each pay cycle verifying penalty rates were correct.

### 4. Inventory management built for product-based businesses

MYOB tracks inventory across multiple locations from day one. You can transfer stock between warehouses, set reorder points, and track serial numbers without paying for add-ons.

Xero's inventory features are basic. You can track quantities, but you'll need third-party apps for serial numbers, batch tracking, or multi-location management. Each app costs $20-50 per month extra.

MYOB's purchase order system connects directly to supplier invoices. You can see what's on order, what's received, and what's outstanding in one view. This matters when you're managing cash flow and need to know exactly what you've committed to pay.

### 5. Support that understands your business

MYOB's support team is based in Australia. Call them and you'll speak to someone who knows what a BAS is, understands Award rates, and can help with SuperStream issues.

Support response times matter when you're trying to process payroll or file your BAS before the deadline. MYOB answers phone calls in under 5 minutes on average. Email responses come back within 4 hours during business days.

Xero's support is spread across global offices. You'll get help, but you'll often need to explain Australian-specific requirements. Phone support isn't included in standard plans.

### But switching isn't free

Moving from Xero to MYOB takes time. You'll need to:
- Export your data from Xero
- Import and verify transactions in MYOB
- Set up your chart of accounts to match
- Train your team on the new interface
- Notify your accountant

Budget 10-20 hours for a complete migration. MYOB offers migration services, but they cost $500-1,500 depending on your data volume.

Most businesses complete the switch over a financial year end when they're already reviewing accounts. This minimizes disruption and gives you clean books in both systems.
